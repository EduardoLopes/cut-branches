Branch rows now carry their actions (set as current, lock or unlock, view the diff, show in history) as GridList row actions: icon buttons over the date on hover or focus, the full list on right-click or Shift+F10, a ⋯ menu on touch, and one-key shortcuts on the focused row (`s`, `l`, `d`, `h`). The actions step aside while branches are selected, since the bulk bar acts then.
